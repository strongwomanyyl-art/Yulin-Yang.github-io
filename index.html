个人简介

目前就读于山东大学海洋学院生物与医药专业， 攻读专业学位硕士研究生。

硕士阶段主要围绕 水产动物品质评价及其分子机制 开展研究。 以不同产地和养殖模式下的鲍鱼为主要研究对象， 系统评价其营养组成、风味特征及热加工品质变化， 并结合蛋白质组学和代谢组学手段， 从分子层面解析水产动物品质差异及其形成机制。

在研究过程中， 积累了水产动物样品处理、 品质与营养指标检测、 多组学数据分析及分子生物学实验经验。 同时参与过裂解性噬菌体分离鉴定、 裂解酶基因挖掘及异源表达研究。

此外， 具有海参实验饲料制备、 室内海参养殖系统搭建、 日常养殖管理及水产动物精准取样等实践经验。

博士阶段希望进一步开展 甲壳动物及其他水产动物营养品质形成与调控 相关研究， 并将养殖实验、 分子生物学及多组学分析相结合， 深入探索水产动物营养、品质及健康相关的分子机制。

Academic Profile · Yulin Yang

About Me

I am currently pursuing a professional master's degree in Biology and Medicine at the Marine College of Shandong University.

My master's research focuses on aquatic animal quality evaluation and the molecular mechanisms underlying quality formation. My research mainly investigates nutritional, flavor-related and processing quality differences among abalone originating from different geographic regions and aquaculture systems.

I integrate conventional nutritional and quality evaluation with proteomics and metabolomics to investigate the molecular basis of aquatic product quality.

I also have research experience in molecular microbiology, including bacteriophage isolation, whole-genome sequencing, molecular cloning and heterologous expression of bacteriophage lysins.

In addition, I have hands-on experience in experimental sea cucumber feed preparation, construction of indoor aquaculture systems, routine aquaculture management and aquatic animal sampling.

For my Ph.D. study, I hope to further investigate the formation and regulation of nutritional quality in crustaceans and other aquatic animals by integrating aquaculture experiments, molecular biology and multi-omics approaches.

Research Experience

Quality Analysis and Multi-omics Evaluation of Abalone under Different Aquaculture Modes

Main Master's Research Project

  • Investigated abalone from different geographic origins and aquaculture systems.
  • Measured conventional nutritional components, including moisture, protein, lipid and ash, together with flavor-related quality indicators.
  • Evaluated quality changes following thermal processing.
  • Applied proteomics and metabolomics for data preprocessing, differential analysis, statistical visualization and functional association analysis.
  • Investigated molecular mechanisms underlying quality differences associated with aquaculture environment and culture mode.

Isolation and Characterization of Lytic Bacteriophages and Heterologous Expression of Phage Lysins

  • Isolated and purified a lytic bacteriophage from environmental samples.
  • Conducted bacteriophage culture, whole-genome sequencing and genome annotation.
  • Identified potential lysin genes based on genomic information.
  • Constructed recombinant expression vectors using molecular cloning techniques.
  • Performed heterologous expression and purification of recombinant lysin protein in Escherichia coli.

Sea Cucumber Experimental Feed Preparation and Indoor Aquaculture System Construction

  • Participated in experimental sea cucumber diet preparation, including ingredient processing, formulation and feed shaping.
  • Participated in construction and organization of an indoor sea cucumber aquaculture facility.
  • Assisted with culture tanks, water supply and exchange systems, and aeration equipment.
  • Gained hands-on experience in feeding, routine management, sample collection and experimental system maintenance.
